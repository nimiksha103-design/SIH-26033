## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution: FRESHFARM 2.0

## 1.Proposed Solution

FRESHFARM 2.0 is an AI-powered digital agricultural marketplace designed to connect farmers directly with consumers, retailers, and bulk buyers without unnecessary intermediaries.

The platform provides three separate interfaces for farmers, buyers, and delivery drivers.

### Major Features:

• Direct Farmer-to-Buyer Connection: Enables farmers to sell their agricultural products directly to customers.

• Smart Vegetable Quantity Suggestion: Calculates the approximate quantity of vegetables required based on the number of people.

• AI-Based Demand Forecasting: Predicts market demand and helps farmers plan their production.

• Smart Logistics Support: Connects farmers with nearby delivery drivers.

• Route Optimization: Helps drivers identify efficient delivery routes.

• Smart Delivery Pricing: Calculates transportation charges based on distance, product weight, and vehicle type.

• Multiple Payment Options: Supports online payments and Cash on Delivery (COD).

• Monthly Expense Dashboard: Displays buyers' monthly purchasing expenses.

• Farmer Earnings Dashboard: Shows farmers' monthly sales, income, and earnings.

• Driver Earnings Dashboard: Tracks drivers' monthly delivery income.

• Bilingual Support: Provides Tamil and English language options.

• SMS and Voice Support: Helps farmers access essential services even through basic mobile phones.

### Technologies to be Used: 

• Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript.

• Backend: Python.

• Database: MySQL.

• AI/ML: Python-based machine learning for demand forecasting.

• Maps and Location Services: GPS and mapping APIs.

• Payment Integration: Online payment gateway and Cash on Delivery.

• Communication: SMS and voice-based services.

### Implementation Process:

• Farmers register and upload their available agricultural products.

• Buyers browse products from nearby farmers.

• Buyers select products and place orders.

• The system calculates the approximate delivery charges.

• Nearby drivers receive delivery requests.

• Drivers accept orders and complete deliveries.

• Buyers make payments through online payment or COD.

• The system automatically updates the monthly expense and earnings dashboards.

## 3.Feasibility and Viability
FRESHFARM 2.0 is technically and economically feasible because it uses commonly available technologies and smartphones.

### Potential Challenges:

• Limited digital knowledge among farmers.

• Transportation and delivery difficulties.

• Internet connectivity issues in rural areas.

• Maintaining product quality during transportation.

• Building trust between farmers and buyers.

### Solutions:

• Simple and user-friendly application design.

• Tamil and English language support.

• SMS and voice-based access.

• Location-based driver connections.

• Secure payment options.

• Product quality and order verification.

• The project can initially be implemented in Tamil Nadu and gradually expanded across India.
